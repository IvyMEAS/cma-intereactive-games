Clinic purchasing rules
  1. Count usable shelf and reserve stock. Keep held stock out of the usable count.
  2. Calculate variance as usable stock minus the ledger count. Confirmed deliveries are still in transit.
  3. For purchasing only, add confirmed incoming units to usable stock. If that total is at or below the reorder point, order enough to reach target stock. Vendors sell full packs only, so round up. Otherwise order zero.
